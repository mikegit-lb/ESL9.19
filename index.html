Grammar Reference Hub: The 4 Core Target Structures

Compare formulas, key time markers, communicative purposes, and common ESL pitfalls in one comprehensive guide.

Grammar Structure Core Formula When to Use in Speaking Key Trigger Words Example Spoken Response
Present Modals Modal + Base Verb Advice, obligation, capability, or present speculation should, must, have to, might, can't be "You should look for a mentor; you must not rush."
Present Perfect have/has + V3 Life experiences, unfinished time, recent news affecting now ever, never, since, for, already, just, yet "I have worked in finance since 2021."
Past Simple V2 / didn't + V1 Finished past actions at a definite, specific time yesterday, in 2019, 3 years ago, last summer "I graduated from university two years ago."
Past Perfect had + V3 An action completed BEFORE another past event (flashback) before, by the time, already, because, had hoped "When I arrived, the meeting had already started."
